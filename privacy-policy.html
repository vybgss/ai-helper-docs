AI Helper - Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 20, 2025

At AI Helper ("we", "us", or "our"), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information while providing you with an exceptional AI chat experience. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our iOS application, AI Helper. By using AI Helper, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 User Identification

AI Helper generates a unique, anonymous user identifier (UUID) that is stored locally on your device. This identifier is used to maintain your chat sessions and does not contain any personally identifiable information.

1.2 Chat Messages

When you send messages through the app, we process the content of your chat messages.

1.3 Subscription Information

If you purchase a premium subscription, Apple handles all payment processing. We receive subscription status information through Apple's StoreKit framework but do not have access to your payment details or personal financial information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use information for the following purposes:

  • Processing and responding to your chat messages through our AI service
  • Verifying subscription status to provide features
  • Providing technical support when needed

3. Data Storage and Transmission

3.1 Local Storage

Your chat messages and user identifier are stored locally on your device. This local data remains under your control and is protected by your device's security measures.

3.2 Server Communication

When you send messages, they are transmitted securely to our API servers for AI processing. We use industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS) to protect data in transit.

Important: Your chat messages are processed by our AI service to generate responses. While we implement security measures, please avoid sharing sensitive personal information such as passwords, social security numbers, or financial details in your conversations.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your data may be shared only in the following limited circumstances:

  • AI Processing: Chat messages are processed by our AI service to generate responses
  • Apple Services: Subscription data is handled through Apple's App Store and StoreKit
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or to protect our rights and safety
  • Service Providers: With trusted partners who assist in app operation under strict confidentiality agreements

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data:

  • Secure HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data transmission
  • Authentication tokens for API access
  • Limited access to user data on a need-to-know basis

6. Data Retention

Local Data: Chat messages stored on your device remain until you delete the app or clear the chat history.
Server Data: We retain message data only as long as necessary to provide the service.

7. Children's Privacy

AI Helper is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed and stored on servers located outside your country of residence.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date.
