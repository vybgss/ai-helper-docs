Quick start

  • Ask anythingType in the message field at the bottom, or tap a suggestion above it, and tap the send arrow.
  • Find your chatsTap the menu button at the top left (or swipe from the left edge on iPhone) to open your history.
  • Try it freeYour first 5 replies are free. After that, All Access keeps the conversation going.

Getting started

What can I ask AI Helper?

Almost anything: explanations, recipes, travel plans, emails, translations, study help, brainstorming or code. Replies are formatted with headings, lists, tables and code blocks so they are easy to read.

Which languages does it support?

AI Helper replies in the language you write in. Write in Ukrainian, Spanish, Japanese or any other language and the answer comes back in the same one. The app's own interface is in English.

Which AI model does the app use?

Replies are generated by OpenAI's GPT-4.1 nano model, guided by AI Helper's instructions to give clear, well-structured answers.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes. On iPad your chat history sits in a sidebar next to the conversation. On iPhone it slides in as a drawer. AI Helper needs iOS 18.5 or later.

Can I change the theme or turn off the typing animation?

Open the history, tap the gear button at the bottom to open Settings, then choose a Theme (System, Light or Dark) or switch off Typing animation to show replies instantly.

Are AI answers always correct?

No. AI can make mistakes or sound confident about something that is wrong. Double-check important facts, and do not rely on AI Helper for medical, legal or financial decisions.

Chats

How do I start a new chat?

Tap the compose button at the top right of the chat, or New chat in the history. Your previous chat stays in the history.

How do I find an older conversation?

Open the history with the menu button at the top left. Chats are grouped by date (Today, Yesterday, Previous 7 Days and so on), and the search field at the top finds chats by their content.

How do I rename, share or delete a chat?

Tap the chat title at the top of the screen for Rename, Share Chat and Delete. In the history, swipe a chat to rename or delete it, or touch and hold it for all three actions.

Can I change a message I already sent?

Touch and hold your message and choose Edit & Resend. The message and everything after it is removed, and its text goes back into the message field so you can change it and send it again.

How do I get a different answer?

Tap the Regenerate button under the latest reply. To stop a reply that is still being written, tap the stop button in the message field.

How do I copy part of a reply?

Use the copy button under a reply to copy all of it. To copy only part, touch and hold the reply, choose Select Text and select what you need. Code blocks also have their own copy button.

Why did a new chat open when I came back?

In Settings → Start new chat you can have AI Helper open a fresh chat when you return after a day or a month. Earlier chats are not deleted; they stay in your history. Choose Never to always continue where you left off.

Are my chats synced between my devices?

No. Your chats are stored only on the device where you had them, and they are not synced through iCloud. To keep a conversation elsewhere, use Share Chat to send it to Notes, Mail or Files.

Subscription

Is AI Helper free?

You can try it with 5 free replies. After that, chatting requires the All Access monthly subscription. Settings shows how many free messages you have left. Replies that fail or that you stop do not use up a free message.

What does All Access include?

Unlimited everyday chatting with advanced, well-structured AI answers, fast responses, priority support and no ads. The price in your currency is shown in the app before you subscribe.

How do I subscribe?

Tap Upgrade at the bottom of the history, the card at the top of Settings, or See plans when you run out of free messages. Payment is handled by Apple and charged to your Apple Account.

I already subscribed. How do I restore it on a new device?

Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account, open the subscription screen and tap Restore.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

In AI Helper, open Settings and tap Manage on the card at the top. You can also go to the iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; you keep access until then.

How do I request a refund?

Apple handles all payments, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue refunds ourselves.

Why do I still have few free messages after reinstalling?

The free message count is kept securely on your device, so deleting and reinstalling the app does not reset it.

Privacy

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no login. AI Helper never asks for your name, email or phone number.

Where are my conversations stored?

Only on your device. When you send a message, the current conversation is sent through our server to OpenAI to generate the reply; our server does not keep it. The Privacy Policy explains the details.

How do I delete my data?

Delete single chats from the chat menu or the history, or remove everything with Settings → Delete all chats. Deleting the app also removes your conversations. Deleted chats cannot be recovered.

Troubleshooting

I see "Something went wrong" instead of a reply

Check that your device is online (Wi-Fi or mobile data, and that AI Helper is allowed to use mobile data in iOS Settings), then tap Try again on the card. If it keeps happening, wait a minute and try again, or contact us.

A reply is taking a long time

Long answers can take a little longer to arrive on a slow connection. You can tap the stop button and send your message again, or try a shorter question.

The app asks me to subscribe even though I already did

Open the subscription screen and tap Restore. Check that you are signed in with the Apple Account you used to subscribe, and in iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions that the subscription is active. If you use Family Sharing with Ask to Buy, the purchase may still be waiting for approval.

The app closes unexpectedly or looks wrong

Update AI Helper to the latest version in the App Store, then close the app and open it again. If the problem continues, restart your device. Please email us with your device model, iOS version and the app version from Settings → Version.
